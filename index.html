My Life Story

1. Birth and Family Background

I was born on 5 January 2008 in a small village called Agwanpur in Meerut district, Uttar Pradesh. My family is a simple middle-class family. My father is a farmer with about 20 bighas of land, and he also does embroidery (ironwork). My mother is a homemaker.

In childhood our situation was normal, but as time passed, the family expenses grew and the sources of income remained limited, so we also saw a period of financial hardship. We are five siblings — three elder brothers, then me, and the youngest is my sister.

2. Education and a Unique Turn

I come from the Scheduled Caste (Jatav) community. Since childhood I have had a deep interest in studies and I believed in understanding the reason behind things rather than just memorising them. My primary education was at Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir in my village. After that, I completed classes 6 to 10 at Genius Public School (A.G.P.S), Agwanpur.

A very interesting turn came in my school education when I passed class 2. My elder brother was one year older than me and was in class 3. According to the rule, I was to go to class 3, but I jumped directly from class 2 to class 4! In this way, my brother and I came into the same class and we studied together till class 12.

I completed classes 11 and 12 (PCM — Physics, Chemistry, Maths) at Gandhi Smarak Inter College, Kila Parikshitgarh (GSDN). Currently I have completed my Graduation (B.A.) from Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), Meerut through private mode.

3. Learning Coding on My Own Without a Computer

I was attracted to technology and mobile phones since childhood. I had neither a computer nor any guide to teach me coding. But I did not give up and learned coding through self-learning using the internet and YouTube on my ordinary smartphone. By the age of 18–19, I had developed several apps and websites on my own. Even with limited pocket money, I bore the expenses of these digital projects.

4. How Did the Idea of Milk Scale App Come About?

In my village Agwanpur, I saw that dairy businessmen and cattle owners spent hours at the end of the month adding up milk accounts, fat and Badhotri. There were also disputes when slips were lost or calculations went wrong.

I thought, why not find an easy digital solution to this rural problem? From this very thought, Milk Scale App was born, which today completes hours of work for dairy owners in just a few clicks.

"I believe that staying in the race only for a government job is not the only success. In today's era, the most sustainable way to earn money is to solve a real problem in the market and sell something. Whether it is a product, a service, or your knowledge. Become self-reliant and develop a business mindset."
